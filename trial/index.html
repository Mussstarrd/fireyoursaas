HVAC Quote Book

A flat-rate price book and quoting tool that lives in one file on your phone. No account, no login, nothing phones home.

Open the Quote Book →

Android phone, Chrome browser. Opens right here in the browser.

Try it in five minutes

  1. Setup tab: put in your own wage, overhead and markups, or leave the sample numbers to start.
  2. Quotes tab: + New quote, type a customer, + Repair, pick from the book. Tap After-hrs and watch the price move.
  3. Lock this quote, then Print / PDF to see the customer copy. Customer signs works with a finger.
  4. Price book tab: change a markup tier in Setup and every price in the book follows.

Keeping your work

  • Tap Save. The first time, your phone asks where to put the file (Downloads is fine). That one file is your Quote Book from then on.
  • After that, Save writes into that file, whether you are on this link or you open the file itself from your Files app.
  • Open the file from Files and it runs without internet. Until you save, what you type stays in this browser on this phone.

What to tell me

  • Anything that confused you, even for a second.
  • A price that looks wrong for your area, and what you would have charged.
  • A repair or part that is missing from the book.
